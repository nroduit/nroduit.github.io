## <center>Crediting the release the results were read on</center>

If Weasis displayed or measured the images behind a result, cite it in the methods section like any
other instrument — at the **version you used**, since releases differ in what they render and how
they compute. The version is shown in the application's **About** window and in the installer file
name. There is no DOI, so the version, the URL and the date you retrieved it carry the reference.

{{< citation >}}

If your setup also relies on ViewerHub, weasis-pacs-connector, weasis-dicom-tools or Karnak, cite
those separately.

Once the work is out, tell us about it: published studies, hospital deployments and teaching
programs that use Weasis are collected in the [Stories](stories) section, and
[Get Involved](get-involved) explains where to send yours. Hearing how the viewer is actually used
is what shapes what gets built next.
